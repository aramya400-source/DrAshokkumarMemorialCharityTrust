# Dr. Ashokkumar Memorial Charity Trust

**Official Website Repository**

Welcome to the official website repository of Dr. Ashokkumar Memorial Charity Trust, Salem, Tamil Nadu, India.

## About the Trust

Dr. Ashokkumar Memorial Charity Trust is dedicated to supporting underprivileged communities through charitable initiatives focused on healthcare, education, food assistance and women empowerment.

## Our Key Activities

- Eye camps and vision care initiatives
- Medical and healthcare support
- Educational assistance for students
- Food distribution and community welfare
- Women empowerment initiatives
- Support for needy families
- Community appreciation programmes
